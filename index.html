What is a typing test?

A typing test measures how quickly and accurately you can type a sample of text. Results are commonly displayed as words per minute, or WPM, together with your accuracy.

What is WPM?

WPM means words per minute. A standard word is generally calculated as five typed characters, allowing typing speed to be compared consistently.

How can I type faster?

Practice regularly, focus on accuracy before speed, use proper finger positioning and gradually increase your typing pace.

TYPING TEST FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good typing speed?

Around 40 WPM is common for everyday typing. Speeds of 60 WPM or more are considered strong for many users.

How is typing accuracy calculated?

Accuracy compares correctly typed characters with the total number of characters typed.

How long should a typing test be?

A one-minute test is convenient for quick checks, while longer tests can provide a more stable measure.
