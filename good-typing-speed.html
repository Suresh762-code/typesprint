What Is a Good Typing Speed?

Understand WPM, common typing-speed ranges and what your score means for everyday computer use.

Quick answer

For many everyday computer users, around 40 WPM is a useful baseline. Around 60 WPM is strong for regular office work, while 80 WPM and above is fast for many typists. Accuracy remains just as important as raw speed.

What does WPM mean?

WPM stands for words per minute. It is the most common measurement used to describe typing speed.

A standard typing word is usually treated as five characters, including spaces and punctuation. This allows typing speed to be compared consistently even when the text contains words of different lengths.

So, what is a good typing speed?

There is no single perfect score for everyone. A good typing speed depends on what you use the keyboard for.

Someone writing occasional emails may be comfortable at a lower speed, while a developer, writer, transcriptionist or administrative professional may benefit from typing much faster.

For most people, improving both speed and accuracy over time is more useful than trying to reach a particular number immediately.

General typing-speed ranges

Typing Speed General Description
Below 30 WPM Developing typing skills
30–40 WPM Comfortable basic typing
40–60 WPM Good everyday typing speed
60–80 WPM Strong typing performance
80–100 WPM Fast typing
100+ WPM Very fast typing performance

These ranges are broad guides rather than strict standards. The difficulty of the text and the required accuracy can significantly affect a score.

Speed is not everything

Typing quickly while making many mistakes is usually less useful than typing slightly slower with high accuracy.

A balanced typing practice routine should therefore track both WPM and accuracy.

When practicing, try to keep your error rate low before pushing for a higher speed.

What affects typing speed?

Several factors can influence your score:

  • Keyboard familiarity
  • Typing technique
  • Text difficulty
  • Punctuation and numbers
  • Test duration
  • Accuracy
  • Fatigue and concentration
  • Keyboard layout and device

How can you improve your typing speed?

Practice regularly

Short, consistent practice sessions are generally more useful than occasional long sessions.

Prioritize accuracy

Try to reduce errors first. Speed often increases naturally as correct typing becomes automatic.

Practice different content

Normal prose is useful, but also practice numbers, punctuation and code if those are relevant to your work.

Track your progress

Compare your WPM and accuracy over time instead of focusing on a single result.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 40 WPM good?

Around 40 WPM can be a practical typing speed for many everyday tasks.

Is 60 WPM good?

Yes. Around 60 WPM is a strong typing speed for many students and professionals.

Is 100 WPM fast?

Yes. Maintaining around 100 WPM with good accuracy is a very fast typing speed for most users.

How often should I test my typing speed?

You can practice daily, but comparing progress over several days or weeks often gives a better picture than repeatedly testing within a few minutes.
