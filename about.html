ABOUT TYPESPRINT

Helping You Type Faster, More Accurately.

TypeSprint is a free online typing platform designed to help students, professionals, developers and everyday computer users measure their typing speed, improve accuracy and build confidence through simple, focused practice.

OUR STORY

A simple idea: make typing practice useful and easy.

TypeSprint was created to provide a straightforward way to test typing speed without unnecessary complexity.

Users can measure words per minute, monitor accuracy, identify typing errors and practice using different modes such as normal text, numbers, punctuation and programming.

The goal is simple: give users practical tools that encourage consistent improvement.
