1. Overview

TypeSprint provides online typing tests, typing practice tools and informational content related to typing speed, accuracy and keyboard skills.

This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be processed when you visit or interact with TypeSprint.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features and services enabled on TypeSprint, certain technical information may be processed automatically.

This may include information such as:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Approximate geographic region
  • Pages visited
  • Referring website or search engine
  • Date and time of website access
  • General website interaction information

Web hosting and security providers may also process basic network information, such as IP addresses, as part of delivering and protecting the website.

3. Browser Local Storage

TypeSprint may use browser local storage to remember certain information directly on your device.

This helps provide useful features without requiring you to create an account.

Personal Best Your best typing score may be stored locally so it can be displayed during future visits.
Theme Preference Your light or dark mode preference may be stored in the browser.

You can normally remove locally stored information by clearing your browser's site data or storage.

4. How Information May Be Used

Information associated with website usage may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain TypeSprint
  • Improve website functionality
  • Understand which features are useful to visitors
  • Detect technical problems
  • Protect the website from abuse or security threats
  • Measure website performance
  • Support advertising where applicable

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

TypeSprint itself may use browser storage or similar technologies to remember preferences and support website functionality.

Third-party services used by TypeSprint may also use cookies or similar technologies where permitted.

Where required by applicable law, visitors may be provided with appropriate cookie or consent choices.

6. Analytics

TypeSprint may use website analytics services in the future to understand general traffic patterns and improve the website.

Analytics services may process information such as pages viewed, device information, browser information, general location and interaction data.

If analytics services are enabled, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate to identify the relevant providers and describe their use.

7. Advertising and Google AdSense

TypeSprint may display advertising to help support the operation and development of the website.

We may use Google AdSense or other advertising services. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure and personalize advertisements where permitted.

Google's advertising services may process information about visits to this website and other websites in accordance with Google's own privacy policies and advertising practices.

Where legally required, TypeSprint will provide appropriate consent controls before using advertising technologies that require consent.

8. Third-Party Services

TypeSprint may rely on third-party providers for services such as:

  • Website hosting
  • Content delivery
  • Security and protection
  • Analytics
  • Advertising

These providers may process technical information necessary to provide their services and may operate under their own privacy policies.

9. External Links

TypeSprint may contain links to external websites.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of third-party websites. Visitors should review the privacy policies of external websites before providing information to them.

10. Children's Privacy

TypeSprint is a general typing practice website and does not intentionally request children to submit personal information through the typing test itself.

If additional account, communication or data-collection features are introduced in the future, appropriate privacy safeguards will be considered.

11. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures may be used to protect TypeSprint and information processed through the website.

However, no website or internet transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your browser and location, you may have options to manage:

  • Cookies
  • Browser local storage
  • Advertising preferences
  • Analytics or consent settings

Browser settings can normally be used to delete cookies and stored website data.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as TypeSprint introduces new features, technologies, analytics providers, advertising services or legal requirements.

The latest revision date will appear near the top of this page.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or privacy practices related to TypeSprint, please use our Contact page.
